## 🎯 Sprint Objective

Analyze the 36 completed Sprint 1 baseline reports to:
1. **Interpret data objectively** - What do the numbers mean in context of a WebGL experiential site?
2. **Identify patterns** - What correlations exist across technical, design, and strategic findings?
3. **Document trade-offs** - What intentional design decisions were made and why?
4. **Generate insights** - What actionable opportunities exist for optimization?

---

## 📋 CONTEXT: DESIGN TOLERANCE ACKNOWLEDGMENT

> **IMPORTANT**: Corn Revolution is an experiential WebGL site that **intentionally** prioritizes immersive experience. Per Awwwards jury commentary (July 2020): "Great smooth experience, really a masterpiece." 
> 
> This site has **documented tolerance** for:
> - Performance metrics lower than typical websites (acceptable for heavy WebGL)
> - Accessibility limitations inherent in canvas-based WebGL experiences
> 
> **All analysis must be OBJECTIVE** - documenting facts without judgment, acknowledging that trade-offs are **intentional creative decisions**, not oversights.

## ⚠️ OBJECTIVITY GUIDELINES

### DO:
✅ State facts: "Lighthouse Performance score is 42"  
✅ Acknowledge context: "For a WebGL experiential site, this is within expected range"  
✅ Quantify: "Load time increased by 45% under 3G conditions"  
✅ Cross-reference: "This correlates with finding A1-02 about draw calls"

### DON'T:
❌ Make judgments: "This is bad/poor/inadequate"  
❌ Ignore context: Treating this like a utility website  
❌ Speculate without evidence: "They should have..."  
❌ Compare unfairly: Against non-WebGL sites

### REMEMBER:
> "Corn Revolution won Awwwards Site of the Year 2020 with jury scores of Design 8.9, Usability 8.2, Creativity 9.1, Content 8.5, Developer 8.7. The trade-offs documented here are INTENTIONAL DESIGN DECISIONS that contributed to this recognition, not oversights to be corrected."
