## 📊 BUSINESS METRICS REFERENCE

### 1. ROI Calculation Framework

#### Basic ROI Formula

```
ROI = ((Revenue from Experience - Cost of Experience) / Cost of Experience) × 100

Example:
Revenue Generated: $500,000
Total Cost: $150,000
ROI = (($500,000 - $150,000) / $150,000) × 100 = 233%
```

#### Cost Components

| Category | Components | Typical Range |
|----------|------------|---------------|
| **Development** | Design, development, QA | 50-60% of budget |
| **Infrastructure** | Hosting, CDN, security | 5-10% of budget |
| **Marketing** | Promotion, advertising | 20-30% of budget |
| **Maintenance** | Updates, support | 10-15% annually |

#### Revenue Attribution

| Source | Calculation | Weight |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Direct Conversions | Lead value × Conversions | 100% |
| Assisted Conversions | Lead value × 0.5 × Assists | 50% |
| Brand Value | Awareness lift × Reach | Variable |
| Content Leverage | Cost savings vs alternatives | 100% |

### 2. Lead Value Calculation

#### B2B Lead Value Formula

```
Lead Value = Deal Size × Close Rate × (Attribution %)

Example:
Average Deal Size: $50,000
Average Close Rate: 15%
Attribution: 40% to experience
Lead Value = $50,000 × 0.15 × 0.40 = $3,000 per lead
```

#### Lead Scoring Integration

| Lead Score | Definition | Value Multiplier |
|------------|------------|------------------|
| Hot (80-100) | High intent, qualified | 1.5× |
| Warm (50-79) | Engaged, some qualification | 1.0× |
| Cold (0-49) | Early stage, unqualified | 0.5× |

#### Primary Business Metrics

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement |
|-----|------------|--------|-------------|
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)** | Total spend / Leads | Industry: $100-500 | Monthly |
| **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** | Total spend / Customers | Varies by product | Monthly |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** | Total customer revenue | >3× CAC | Quarterly |
| **Payback Period** | Time to recover CAC | <12 months | Quarterly |

#### Experience-Specific KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement |
|-----|------------|--------|-------------|

### 8. Forecasting Models

#### Lead Generation Forecast

```
Monthly Leads = Traffic × CVR × Seasonality Factor

Example:
Monthly Traffic: 50,000
CVR: 3%
January Seasonality: 0.8
Expected Leads = 50,000 × 0.03 × 0.8 = 1,200 leads
```

| Month | Factor | Notes |
