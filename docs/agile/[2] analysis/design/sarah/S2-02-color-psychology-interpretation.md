## 🎨 COLOR PSYCHOLOGY ANALYSIS

### Primary Colors and Meaning

**Green/Yellow Tones** (Corn/Agriculture):
- Associations: Growth, nature, agriculture, renewal
- Brand alignment: Perfect for agricultural product
- Emotional impact: Positive, optimistic, natural

**Earth Tones**:
- Associations: Stability, reliability, tradition
- Brand alignment: Agricultural heritage
- Emotional impact: Trustworthy, grounded

### Color Progression Through Narrative

**Introduction (0-20%)**: Fresh, vibrant greens
- Represents new beginning, innovation

**Growth (20-60%)**: Deepening, maturing colors
- Represents development, progress

**Conclusion (60-100%)**: Rich, golden tones
- Represents achievement, harvest, success

**Interpretation**: Color journey mirrors corn lifecycle. Psychologically reinforces narrative progression.
