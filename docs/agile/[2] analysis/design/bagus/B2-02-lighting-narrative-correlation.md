## 💡 LIGHTING-NARRATIVE ANALYSIS

### Lighting Progression Through Narrative (From B1-02, A1-04)

**Introduction (0-20% scroll)**:
- Lighting: Soft, even illumination
- Mood: Welcoming, clear
- Narrative purpose: Establish baseline, introduce subject

**Development (20-60% scroll)**:
- Lighting: Dynamic changes, dramatic angles
- Mood: Energetic, progressive
- Narrative purpose: Show transformation, growth

**Climax/Environment (60-80% scroll)**:
- Lighting: Varied environmental conditions
- Mood: Realistic, context-dependent
- Narrative purpose: Show resilience, adaptation

**Conclusion (80-100% scroll)**:
- Lighting: Warm, golden hour quality
- Mood: Achievement, completion
- Narrative purpose: Harvest, success, fulfillment

**Interpretation**: Lighting progression mirrors emotional and narrative arc. Professional cinematic techniques applied.

### Lighting as Storytelling Tool

**Techniques Observed** (From B1-02):
1. **Color Temperature Shifts**: Cool to warm = journey progression
2. **Intensity Variation**: Dramatic moments = higher contrast
3. **Direction Changes**: Camera angle coordination = cinematic quality
4. **Shadow Usage**: Depth and drama enhancement

**Assessment**: Lighting is NOT just illumination—it's active narrative tool. Design score 8.9/10 reflects this sophistication.
