## Recommendations for Zenotika x UNIKOM

### Business Case Template

```yaml
1. Define Success Metrics:
   - Lead generation target
   - Engagement goals
   - Brand lift objectives

2. Calculate Investment:
   - Development: $X
   - Design: $Y  
   - Infrastructure: $Z
   - Total: $X+Y+Z

3. Project Revenue Impact:
   - Leads expected: N
   - Conversion rate: C%
   - Avg deal value: $V
   - Revenue = N × C% × $V

4. Compute ROI:
   - ROI = (Revenue - Investment) / Investment
   - Payback period = Investment / (Revenue/year)

5. Add Intangible Benefits:
   - Brand value
   - Media coverage
   - Competitive advantage
```
