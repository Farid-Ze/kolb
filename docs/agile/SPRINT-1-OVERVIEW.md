## 🎯 CONTEXT & APPROACH

### About Corn Revolution
Corn Revolution is an **experiential WebGL site** by Resn and Bader Rutter that intentionally prioritizes immersive experience over traditional performance/accessibility metrics. This is a documented creative decision, not an oversight (Source: Awwwards jury commentary, July 2020).

### Analysis Approach
All findings must be documented **objectively** without judgment, acknowledging the trade-offs made by the development team. The goal is to understand and document the technical implementation, not to critique design decisions.
